02 · About

The team

3 girls

Odd One Out is Kinga, Jula and Gabi — three friends who met studying game development at PJATK in Warsaw and kept making games together long after the deadlines ran out.

We have shipped a 2D platformer in six weeks, a tower defense in 3D, a game jam in one night, a Roblox game with real players, and a thesis that will eat a year. Everything on this page was built as a team: we design, code, draw and model it ourselves.
