Why no engine

Engines hide the hard parts: the frame loop, drawing things in the right order, turning key presses into movement, deciding when two things touch. Writing a game without one means writing all of that yourself.

That is the point. After shipping games in Unity and Roblox, building one from the ground up shows what is actually underneath — and that we can rebuild it when an engine gets in the way.
