A slot machine for the browser. Spinning reels, paylines, win animations, a payout table and the hundred small timings that make a spin feel satisfying instead of random.

The work here is frontend design: layout that holds together at any width, reel animation that stays smooth, state that never lets you spin twice at once, and the celebratory juice when the symbols line up.

Level 06 · Escape room

Halloween Escape Room

Media coming soon
Gameplay · Escape roomSoon

A haunted room you have to think your way out of. Click around, find clues, combine them, open the right lock with the right key — in the right order.

The interesting part is the puzzle logic: a dependency graph of what unlocks what, state that survives wrong guesses, hints that appear only when you are actually stuck, and a layout where every object is a clickable hotspot.
